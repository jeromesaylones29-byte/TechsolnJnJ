Order processing

We verify and pack every order by hand. Please allow 1–2 business days for processing before dispatch. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

Order cut-off

Orders placed before 2:00 PM on a business day are usually packed the same day. Anything after that roll-over is packed the following business day.

Tracking your order

Once your parcel is handed to the courier you will receive a tracking number by email. You can use it on the courier's site to follow your delivery in real time.

Delivery problems

  • Late parcel — wait 1 business day past the estimate, then message us with your order number
  • Marked delivered but missing — check with neighbors and the front desk first, then contact us
  • Arrived damaged — keep all packaging and take photos before contacting us

Returns & Exchanges

Request a return

Tell us what happened and we will issue your RMA number.

Return window & eligibility

You may request a return within 7 days of the delivery date. To qualify for a refund or exchange the item must meet all of the following:

  • Unused and in the same condition you received it
  • Complete with all original accessories, cables, manuals and mounting hardware
  • Returned in its original undamaged packaging with any seals intact
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or receipt)

Faulty, damaged or dead-on-arrival

If a part arrives damaged, is missing pieces, or fails within the return window, contact us immediately with photos. We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund, and we cover the return shipping cost.

Change of mind

If you simply changed your mind, you may return an unopened, unused item within 7 days. In this case a restocking / return-shipping fee may apply, and the refund covers the item value only — not the original delivery fee.

Not eligible for return

  • Software, digital licences and downloadable keys
  • Items with broken security seals or removed serial labels
  • Custom-built or configured systems
  • Damage caused by misuse, liquid, overclocking or physical impact
  • Consumables such as thermal paste once opened

Warranty vs. returns

The 7-day window covers returns and exchanges. Faults after that window fall under each product's manufacturer warranty (for example 6 or 12 months) — we can help you file a warranty claim with the brand.

How to start a return

1

Request an RMA. Email us your order number and the reason for the return. We reply with a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number.

2

Pack it up. Repack the item with all accessories and original packaging, and write the RMA number on the outside of the box.

3

Ship it back. We send you the return address and instructions. Drop the parcel at the courier and keep the tracking receipt.

4

Inspection. Once received, our team inspects the item against our return conditions (usually within 1–2 business days).
