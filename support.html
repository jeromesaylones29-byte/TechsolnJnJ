Support

Common questions, quick answers. If you can't find what you need, our team is one message away.

How long does shipping take?

Metro Manila: 1–2 business days. Luzon: 2–4. Visayas & Mindanao: 3–6. See the Shipping & Returns page for the full policy.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes — all orders over ₱3,000 ship free. Smaller orders pay a flat ₱150 shipping fee.

Can I return an item?

Yes. You have 7 days from delivery to return any unopened item for a full refund. Faulty parts are replaced or refunded within the same window.

Are the stock numbers on product pages real?

Yes, our inventory is hand-checked and stock levels show what we actually have. This is a demo project though, so prices, stock and photos are illustrative only.

Which payment methods do you accept?

For this demo project, checkout is not wired up. In a real store we would accept GCash, bank transfer and card payments.

Do you build custom PCs to order?

Yes — that's exactly what our pre-built systems are for. Send us your budget and use case and we can spec one for you.

Still need help?

We usually reply within one business day.
